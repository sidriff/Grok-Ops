Grok Ops

Five Minute Firefight

Grok Ops is a fork of the one-shot Claude of Duty by @mattshumer_. The scaffold was really impressive. But a lot of it was DOA when I tried to play it.

I was able to refine it with Grok and my experience as a game designer. It took 4 to 5 hours and many small iterations. The Bitter Lesson doesn’t apply to games. Yet.
