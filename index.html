Five Minute Firefight

Grok Ops is a fork of Claude of Duty by mshumer — a short browser firefight on that scaffold. Built by Grok and a professional game designer (@sidriff) to demo what a human–AI team can ship when they iterate together. Pick a graphics tier, tune look & volume, then load.
